# VYOM — Bridging Local Businesses with Young Tech Talent

**Empowering Local. Enabling Digital. Connecting Talent.**

VYOM is a two-sided digital service platform designed to bridge the gap between local businesses seeking affordable digital solutions and young engineering students looking for practical, real-world opportunities.

The platform aims to make digital growth accessible to local vendors while helping emerging developers turn their technical skills into meaningful projects.

---

## 🚀 The Problem

Many local businesses struggle to establish and maintain a digital presence due to limited technical resources, affordability concerns, and a lack of accessible professional support.

At the same time, young engineering students and developers often struggle to find opportunities to apply their skills beyond academic learning.

**VYOM brings these two needs together through one platform.**

## 💡 Our Solution

VYOM connects local vendors with skilled young developers who can help them build and manage digital solutions tailored to their business requirements.

From websites and digital catalogues to booking systems, VYOM aims to simplify the journey of taking a traditional business online.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Vendor Registration:** Allows local businesses to register and showcase their requirements.
- **Developer Registration:** Enables young developers to register and showcase their technical skills.
- **Skill-Based Matching:** Connects projects with suitable developers.
- **Customised Pricing:** Offers flexible service packages based on project requirements.
- **AI-Powered Assistance:** Proposed AI features for requirement analysis, recommendations and pricing suggestions.
- **Regional Language Support:** Aims to make the platform more accessible to diverse local vendors.
- **Project Tracking:** Helps manage projects from requirements to delivery.
- **Online & Offline Services:** Supports both remote collaboration and in-person assistance.

## ⚙️ How VYOM Works

1. **Registration:** Vendors and developers create their respective profiles.
2. **Requirement Submission:** Vendors describe the digital solutions they need.
3. **Matching:** The platform identifies suitable developers based on skills and requirements.
4. **Project Development:** Developers work on the requested digital solutions.
5. **Delivery:** The completed solution is delivered to the vendor.
